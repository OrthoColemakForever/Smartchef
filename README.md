# SmartChef

A voice-first cooking assistant and recipe database tablet app.

## About

The core idea: add recipes to your digital cookbook and your AI-assistant will answer questions and offer guidance like a professional chef. Forgot what step comes next? Missing an ingredient and not sure what you can substitute? Need a recipe suggestion informed by your personal tastes? Just say the word! Why waste time hunting for answers when you could converse with your kitchen and get a quick response?
